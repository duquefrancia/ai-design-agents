# Glossary · Glosario

The skills and notebooks are written in Spanish (Spain). This glossary maps the key terms. · Las skills y los cuadernos están en español de España. Este glosario recoge los términos clave.

## Approval states · Estados de aprobación

| Español | English | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Apto | Approved | Passes, with the critical aspects verified |
| Apto con observaciones | Approved with observations | Passes; observations do not block |
| Pendiente de verificación | Pending verification | Essential evidence is missing; approval is withheld |
| No apto | Not approved | A demonstrated problem prevents approval |
| La aprobación queda retenida | Approval is withheld | Explains *Pending verification* (not part of its name) |
